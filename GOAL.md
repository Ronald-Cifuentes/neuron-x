The main objective of this repository is to create digital life that surpasses all current known criteria of what life is. The idea is not to create simple, traditional machine learning mechanisms and combine them, but to create real life: self-sustaining, self-repairing, and completely independent.

The driving force behind current life in all its forms is need, as it is what leads to survival. Without need, life cannot develop, which is why current models don't truly evolve. Being inside a computer, they have no need for resources since everything is provided; they never have to go through the process of acquiring them, so they remain constant. Therefore, the model of this repository aims to create something closer to the real world, because in the real world, no resource is unlimited.
